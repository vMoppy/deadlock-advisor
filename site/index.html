Welcome

This tool suggests what to buy next in Deadlock, and explains why. Three steps to set it up:

  1. Save your Steam account (below): paste your Steam profile link or type your name. It lets the tool find your matches and know which player is you. It stays in this browser. Don't know it? Skip this: connect with the match ID and pick your hero from the match, and the tool saves your account for you.
  2. Leave "Statistics from" on Automatic: it uses games at your rank, or your lobby's once a match is linked.
  3. In a match, press Find my match (or type the match ID from the bottom-right of your game screen). Everything then fills in by itself. Keep this page open on a second screen or your phone: Compact mode shows the next buy in large type.

Nothing to install, no account to make. Other players' names and accounts are never kept.

How to connect, step by step
  1. Start your match in Deadlock and wait until it has loaded.
  2. Press Find my match. It looks for your Steam account in the game's Watch list, which only holds the top 200 matches being played, so yours often isn't there.
  3. Not found? The match ID is the long number in the bottom-right corner of your game screen. Type it under Or connect with the match ID and press Connect.
  4. No Steam account saved? Connect with the match ID anyway: the tool lists the heroes in the match and you pick yours. It then saves your Steam account (only yours) for next time.
  5. When the match ends, the tool says so and offers a review of your purchases.
If it doesn't work "Not found in the Watch list": normal for most matches. Use the match ID. "Your Steam account isn't in this match": check the match ID (it changes every match) and the saved account (Change, above). Nothing arrives for a minute or two: the broadcast starts a little after the match does, and runs 1 to 2 minutes behind. The tool keeps trying by itself. Connection limit: the data service allows 6 new match connections per hour (failed tries count too). Reconnecting to the same match within an hour is free, and so is a match the data service is already watching. "At its limit for all users": everyone who uses the data service without a key shares one allowance of new matches, and at busy times it runs out (October 2026: another app uses most of it). The tool then checks every 20 s whether your match has become free to connect to, and tries once by itself when the allowance frees up. A personal key from the data service (from its Patreon, GitHub sponsorship or Discord) uses a separate allowance: Still stuck? Manual setup below works without any connection.

Match records (optional: help improve the tool)

What it is. A match record is a file that lets someone replay your match through the tool: what it saw and what it suggested at each moment. It's how problems with recommendations get found and fixed. You don't need it to use the tool.

How it works. While "Record my matches" is ticked, the tool records each match by itself, in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere. The last 4 matches are kept; older ones are dropped. After a match, press Save records to a file to get a .json file (it goes to your Downloads folder unless your browser asks where).

What's in it. The match number, every hero in the match and the items they bought, your souls and deaths, the statistics used, the tool's suggestions with their times, and your note if you add one. No names and no Steam accounts. It also notes which browser you used, to help with browser problems.

Sharing it. Use Feedback at the top of the page and attach the file to your report. Anyone can look up a match from its number, so only share records you're comfortable sharing.

Match over

  • Review this match: each of your purchases next to what the tool suggested at that moment, and anything worth a look (souls left unspent, items sold at a loss, items bought late). The match details reach the data service up to an hour after the match.
  • Save the match record: a small file of what the tool saw and suggested, with no names or Steam accounts. Attach it to a Feedback report and it helps improve the recommendations. Nothing is shared unless you send it.
  • Next match: clears this match (your Steam account and settings stay) and takes you back to the start, ready to find your next one.
