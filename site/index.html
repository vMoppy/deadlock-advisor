Steam account:

How finding your match works Find my match checks the in-game Watch list for your Steam account. That list only holds the top 200 matches being played, so most matches aren't in it. When yours isn't, type the match ID shown in the bottom-right corner of your screen once the match has loaded, then press Connect. Connecting uses one of the 6 match connections the data service allows per hour (failed tries count too). Reconnecting to the same match within an hour is free. Live data comes from the match broadcast, which runs about 1 to 2 minutes behind your game. It shows what the scoreboard shows: heroes, teams, lanes, items and souls. Keep looking for my next match checks the Watch list every 20 seconds while this page is open and connects by itself when your match shows up. It only helps for matches in the Watch list.

Match records

For testing and improving the tool

While this is on, the tool keeps a record of each match on this computer: the heroes, items and souls it saw, what it recommended and when, and what you bought. Nothing is sent anywhere, and other players' Steam accounts aren't recorded. Save the records to a file to share them.
